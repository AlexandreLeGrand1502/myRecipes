Apple cinnamon roll!

Ingrédients :

  • 1 cup plus 2 tablespoons warm milk (90 degrees F/32 degrees C)
  • 2 1/4 teaspoons active dry yeast
  • 1/3 cup white sugar
  • 6 tablespoons unsalted butter, melted
  • 1 large egg, beaten
  • 3/4 teaspoon fine salt
  • 4 1/4 cups all purpose flour

Etapes :

  1. Pour warm milk into a small bowl and sprinkle yeast on top. Allow to sit for 10 minutes. Add sugar, melted butter, egg, and salt. Whisk to combine. Add flour and stir with a wooden spoon handle until everything is well combined and a sticky dough forms. Cover and let sit for 20 minutes.
  2. Step 2 : Transfer dough to a work surface and press out the air. Grab dough from the edge and stretch it over the top to the center. Repeat about 5 times, going all the way around, until the dough comes together. Flip dough over and use your hand and a bench scraper to form the dough into a tight ball using a circular motion on the table. Set dough into an oiled resealable bag and refrigerate overnight.
  3. Step 3 : The next day transfer cold dough to a floured surface and press flat into a rectangle shape. Dust with flour and roll into a large oval. Dust again and flip dough over. Use you hands to gently stretch the dough to form a rectangle. Continue rolling until you have a rectangle that is approximately 18x12 inches.
